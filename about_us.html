Our purpose

Who We Are

The Chamber of Commerce Bhuj is a leading business association dedicated to promoting trade, industry, and economic development in the Kutch region. We serve as the unified voice of businesses across diverse sectors, from traditional crafts and manufacturing to modern services and technology.

Our mission is to empower local businesses through advocacy, networking opportunities, and practical support. We work closely with government bodies, industry partners, and community organizations to create an environment where businesses can thrive and contribute to regional prosperity.

Whether you're an established enterprise or a growing startup, the Chamber is here to support your journey.
